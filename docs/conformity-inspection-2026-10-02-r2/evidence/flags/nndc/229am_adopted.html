General Comments:

2015De22: 229Am produced in multinucleon transfer reaction 248Cm(48Ca,X),E(48Ca)=270 MeV at the UNILAC facility at GSI. Target=460 μg/cm2 thick 248Cm oxide deposited on Ti backing. The velocity filter SHIP was used to separate target-like reaction products which were implanted on a position sensitive silicon strip detector (PSSD). α box of six Si detectors surrounded the PSSD to detect escape α and SF events. The 229Am nuclide was identified by measuring α energies and half-lives in two decay chains, chain 1: 229Am [Eα=7.99 MeV 2, T1/2=0.9 s +21-7]-> 225Np [Eα=pileup, T1/2=3.3 ms +76-27] -> 221Pa -> 217Ac -> 213Fr [Eα=escape, T1/2=16 s +37-13] and chain 2: 233Bk [Eα=7.77 MeV 2, T1/2=21 s +48-17] -> 229Am [Eα=8.00 MeV 2, T1/2=6.4 s +149-54] -> 225Np [Eα=pileup, T1/2=3.8 ms +76-27] -> 221Pa -> 217Ac -> 213Fr [Eα=6.75 MeV 2, T1/2=110 s +250-90]

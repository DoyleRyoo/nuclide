General Comments:

294Og observed only in experiments at FLNR-JINR-Dubna, in collaboration with LLNL and ORNL laboratories in USA.
2006Og05, 2004Og10, 2004Og12 (also 2009Og07,2007Og05,2005Og03, 2005OgZZ,2004Og05,2003OgZZ,2002OgZX): discovery of Z=118 (Og) element through the identification of 294Og nuclide decaying by three successive α-decay chains: 290Lv, 286Fl and 282Cn, which decays by SF. 294Og from 249Cf(48Ca,3n),E=251 MeV; >98% enriched 249Cf rotating target. The compound nucleus 297Og formed at 32.1-36.6 MeV. Facility: U400 cyclotron with the Dubna gas-filled recoil separator (DGFRS) of the Flerov Laboratory of Nuclear Reactions (FLNR), Joint Institute for Nuclear Research in Dubna (JINR), in collaboration with LLNL, USA. Evaporation residues recoiling from the target were separated by the DGFRS in flight from 48Ca beam ions, transfer reaction products and scattered particles. Multiwire proportional counter for tof measurement; semiconductor focal-plane detector array with 12 vertical position-sensitive strips surrounded by eight side detectors without position sensitivity. The α particles were measured in the range of 9.9 MeV to 12.0 MeV. FWHM=70-120 keV for α particles absorbed in the focal-plane detector, 280-410 keV for α particles that escaped the focal-plane detector and were registered by the side detector.
History of three events observed: two correlated decay chains detected in the experiment at E(48Ca=251 MeV) reported by 2006Og05, and one correlated decay chain in an earlier experiment (2004Og10) at E(48Ca=245 MeV). 2004Mo15 pointed out that the mass assignment in their work may be off by one unit. 2005St38 analyzed probability of observing a random event in their work.

2012Og06 (also 2011Og07): 249Cf(48Ca,3n),E=251.7 MeV; 249Cf from decay of 249Bk target; rotating 249Bk radioactive target. 249Bk radioactive target was produced in neutron irradiation of Cm and Am targets in high flux isotope reactor at ORNL. The chemically purified 12.7 mg of 249Bk (about half of the total 249Bk material produced at ORNL) containing 0.51 mg of 249Cf with no other detectable impurities was shipped to JINR. Six targets were prepared at RIAR facility in Russia, each target with a thickness of 0.33 mg/cm2 deposited on 0.72 mg/cm2 thick Ti foils, and mounted on a disk that was rotated at 1700 rpm perpendicular to the beam direction. U-400 cyclotron with the Dubna gas-filled recoil separator (DGFRS) of the Flerov Laboratory of Nuclear Reactions, Joint Institute for Nuclear Research in Dubna (JINR). Evaporation residues passing through the DGFRS separator registered by time-of-flight system. Focal plane detector system: two 6x6 cm2 Si detectors, each of 16 strips with position sensitivity in the vertical direction. These detectors were surrounded by six similar 6x6 cm2 Si side detectors without position sensitivity. Behind the focal plane detectors, two veto Si detectors, similar to the side detectors, were mounted for detecting or rejecting signals from high-energy long-range charged particles produced in direct reactions of 48Ca with the DGFRS materials. Measured Eα, half-lives, α-α correlations. FWHM for α particles was 34-73 keV. Collaboration: JINR and RIAR, Russia; LLNL, ORNL, University of Tennessee and Vanderbilt university.

2018Br13: E(48Ca)=252,258 MeV beam from U400 cyclotron of JINR-Dubna was incident on a mixed Cf target (50.6% 249Cf, 13.1% 250Cf and 36.3% 252Cf) obtained from >30 year old 252Cf samples at ORNL. The Cf target was fabricated at ORNL as 12 segments, each with a layer of 309 μg/cm2 thick Cf, containing a total of 1 mg of mixed Cf source. Fragments were separated by Dubna Gas-filled recoil separator (DGFRS), and ions were tagged with time-of-flight (tof) in two pentane filled multi-wire proportional counters (MWPC). Focal plane detector array for the detection of α particles, designed and assembled at ORNL, was a double-sided silicon detector (DSSD) consisting of 48 horizontal strips on front side and 128 vertical strips on the back side for position resolution. The escaped α particles were detected in a box of six Si detectors surrounding the focal plane detector. α veto detector was placed behind the focal plane detector to reject light particles. Calibration of the detector system and assessment of target condition was carried out using Yb(48Ca,xn) reaction producing α-decaying isotopes of 216Th to 220Th. Measured recoil-α correlated events, and production cross section. Deduced one event assigned to the decay of 294Og through 294Og |) 290Lv |) 286Fl. Reevaluated T1/2 of 294Og decay, and Eα emitted in this decay by combining the previous data for four observed events and the one in the present work. Comparison with several theoretical predictions of half-lives of α decay of 294Og and its Q(α).

Directions for Using Table of Nuclides
  1. Locate the desired nuclide by dragging the chart or the horizontal slider.

  2. Click the nuclide to see its nuclear property and list of evaluations.

  3. On the list of evaluated nuclear data libraries, click the '+' sign to see the list of available reactions for the desired evaluation.

  4. Click the reaction name to see the plot. To compare the two or more plots, click the 'Add to XSViewer' button for the desired plots and then click the 'Open XSViewer' button.

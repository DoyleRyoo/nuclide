General Comments:

$ 271Sg has been observed as the α-decay daughter of 275Hs at JINR (2004Og12,2006Og05) and GSI (2012Ho12). Chains of α-decaying nuclei were observed which terminated in spontaneous fission. The properties of these chains were compared to the literature in order to assign individual decays to specific nuclei.
$ 2016Ho09 revises chain #1 in 2012Ho12 to begin with 291Lv. An additional α decay was also identified, such that the decay labeled as 277Hs is actually 271Sg.
$ Half-lives, branching ratios, and α-decay energies in this evaluation have been computed from the individual events listed in the references above. Half-life uncertainties have been computed according to the method of 1984Sc13. An additional 10 keV systematic uncertainty is assumed for the α-decay energies, which is added in quadrature to the averaged statistical uncertainty.

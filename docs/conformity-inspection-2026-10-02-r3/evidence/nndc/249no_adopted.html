General Comments:

Two independent experiments, one at FLNR-JINR-Dubna and the other at GSI have identified new 249No nuclide, as described below.
2021Sv02, 2022Te01 (also 2021Te08): 249No produced and identified in 204Pb(48Ca,2n),E=225.4 MeV, followed by separation of evaporation residues using the SHELS velocity filter separator, and detected in the GABRIELA detection system of Double-Sided Silicon Detectors (DSSDs) positioned at the focal plane of the separator, and 5 Ge detectors (1 clover and 4 coaxial Ge detectors). Measured Er-α-α correlated events, Eα, and half-lives of 249No and its daughter product 245Fm, and several events related to α and ε decays of daughters of 245Fm such as 241Cf and 245Es were observed, confirming unambiguously identification of 249No nuclide, although, 2021Sv02 mentioned that more detailed studies were needed to elucidate decay characteristics of 249No nuclide. Experiments were performed at the U400 cyclotron facility of the Flerov Laboratory of Nuclear Reaction (FLNR-JINR-Dubna).
2021Kh07: 249No nuclide identified from the α decay of 253Rf, the latter produced in 204Pb(50Ti,n),E=234.3 MeV, followed by separation of evaporation residues (ERs) using gas-filled TransActinide Separator and Chemistry Apparatus (TASCA) at UNILAC, GSI. Measured (Er)(fission)- and (Er)α-correlated events, Eα, Iα, partial half-lives of two fission activities of 253Rf, α-decay half-life of the newly discovered 249No isotope from the α decay of 253Rf in (Er)-α-α-α correlated events.
Previous (unsuccessful) experiments on the identification of 249No:
2003Be18 (also 2003Po08,2003Ye02): 204Pb(48Ca,2n),E=218-236 MeV; 206Pb(48Ca,4n),E=234.5 MeV. Fragments separated by VASSILISSA mass separator at JINR-Dubna facility. Measured two activities: one assigned to 250No and the other to 249No with half-life of 54 μs +15-10, but with a possibility that both could belong to 250No. Later 2006Pe17, using 204Pb(48Ca,2n),E=220 MeV reaction and Fragment Mass Analyzer at ATLAS-ANL facility assigned the 54-μs activity to 250No. Also 2003Be18 re-investigated the results of their experiment in 2017Sv02 using the same reaction, and concluded that the two activities observed in their 2003 work belonged to 250No and that 249No nuclide was not produced, in agreement with the results obtained by 2006Pe17 at Argonne.
Theoretical nuclear structure calculations:
2012Zh01: calculated bandhead energies of low-lying one-quasiparticle bands, moments of inertia plots of g.s. and excited bands, alignments, occupation probabilities, contribution of neutron and proton major shells to angular momentum alignments using Cranked shell model (CSM), particle-number conserving (PNC) method.
2011Ad15: calculated one-quasiparticle states in n=147 nuclei using Two-center shell model (TCSM), quasiparticle-phonon model (QPM).
2006Sh19: calculated levels, Jπ rotational bands, transition dipole and quadrupole moments using Cluster model.
2005Pa73: calculated neutron single-quasiparticle energies using macroscopic-microscopic approach.
1985Cw01: calculated deformation energy along fission barrier versus β2, fission barrier versus neutron number using Strutinsky method

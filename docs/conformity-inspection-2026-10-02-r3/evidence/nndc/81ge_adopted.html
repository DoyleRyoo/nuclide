E(level):
Jπ(level): 1/2- is expected, based on systematics of Jπ (isomeric state) in other odd-mass n=49 isotones. However, if Jπ=1/2-, B(M4)(W.u.)<1.4 (low cf. values ranging from 7.0 5 to 19 1 for seven known M4 transitions between 1/2- and 9/2+ states (1979En04)). Therefore, 1981Ho24 presume that the 679 level is the 1/2+ level also expected at low energy (based on level energy systematics), and that the 1/2- state is the 896 level which deexcites via an E1 transition to the 679 level.
T1/2(level): the latest measured value of T1/2(g.s.)=6.4 s 2 (2022De07) implies T1/2 |> 7.0 s (from 7.6 s 6 (1981Ho24)) for the isomeric state. 7.6 s 6 in 1981Ho24 (also in 1982FoZZ), authors were unable to distinguish between two similar (g.s. and isomeric) half-lives in their α=81 multispectrum analysis. Presumably other authors’ data also refer to mixtures of the g.s. and isomeric state. Those data are 10.1 s 8 (1972De43), 8.8 s 11 (1974KrZG, 1973KrZN), 7.8 s 2 (1981ZeZY), and 13.4 s (1975Al11).

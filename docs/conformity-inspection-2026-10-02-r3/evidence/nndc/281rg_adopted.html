General Comments:

$ 281Rg has been observed as the α-decay daughter of 285Nh at JINR (2011Og04,2012Og02,2012Og06,2013Og01,2013Og04) and GSI (2019Kh04). Events were identified based on the observation of time- and position-correlated chains of α-decaying nuclei, terminated by spontaneous fission. Comparison of the decay chains to previous observations allowed individual decays to be assigned to specific nuclei.
$ Half-lives, branching ratios, and α-decay energies in this evaluation have been computed from the individual events listed in the references above. Half-life uncertainties have been computed according to the method of 1984Sc13. An additional 10 keV systematic uncertainty is assumed for the α-decay energies, which is added in quadrature to the averaged statistical uncertainty.

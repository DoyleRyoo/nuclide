E(level)Comments
  0Identification of 264Lr isotope by 2022Og07: authors observed α-like events with Eα=7-10 MeV between decays of 272Bh and SF events in 50 out of 55 events, thereby establishing the α decay mode of 268Db for the first time with Eα=7.6-8.0 MeV, together with the discovery of a new isotope 264Lr as α-daughter of 268Db, with half-life determination of 264Lr and its SF decay mode.
E(level): it is assumed that the observed activity corresponds to the ground state of 264Lr.
T1/2(level): from (Er)(SF)-correlated decays in the present experiment (2022Og07).

General Comments:

2022Og07: 264Lr identified as daughter of α-decay of 268Db, which in turn was produced in the following decay chain of 288Mc: 288Mc |) 284Nh |) 280Rg |) 276Mt |) 272Bh |) 268Db. 288Mc itself was produced in 243Am(48Ca,3n),E=239.1, 240.9, 243.9 MeV using a 99.5% enriched target of 243Am, new gas-filled separator DGFRS-2 online to the new cyclotron DC280 at the new Superheavy Element (SHE) Factory, JINR-Dubna. Measured reaction cross sections, evaporation residues (ERs), implants-correlated α and SF decays, Eα, and isotopic half-lives using two double-sided strip detectors (DSSD) with 1 mm wide strips of (BB17 (Ds)-300 semiconductors, and two multiwire proportional counters (MWPC).
Theoretical structure calculations: 1985Cw01 (deformation energy along fission barriers versus β2 and neutron number using Strutinsky method); 2008Do12 (binding energy).
Theoretical calculations of half-lives for α and SF decays of 264Lr: 2021Si02 and 2020Si27 (relativistic mean-field approach); 2020Is03 and 2019Is06 (cluster model); 2019Sr04 (Geiger-Nuttall law); 2017Sa29, 2017Sa63, 2016Sa53 and 2011Sa40 (Coulomb and proximity potential model); 2008Do12 (half-life using a local formula)
Q-value: Estimated uncertainties (2021Wa16): 570 for Q(β-), 490 for S(n), 660 for S(p), 300 for Q(α).
Q-value: S(2p)=11870 480, S(2n)=9870 630 (2021Wa16).

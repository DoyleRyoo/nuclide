    0.0Jπ(level): In 2021Wa39 (186Ta IT Decay), g.s. was assumed as (5-) and proposed Jπ=(9+) for the isomeric state at 347.9. In 2021Ko07 (NUBASE), Jπ=(9+) is proposed for the isomeric state at 336 keV (T1/2=1.54 m) from systematics. The assumption of a Jπ(g.s.)=(5-) conflicts with the data in 186Ta β- decay. For simplicity (3-) is assumed by the evaluators, (2-) is possible but less likely. Possible configuration: ((π 7/2[404]) (ν 3/2[512] or 1/2[510])) (1970Pa16,1973Gu02); log ft=7.5 (log f1ut=8.8) to 3-, log ft=7.5 (log f1ut=8.6) to (2+), log ft=8.2 (log f1ut=9.5) to (2+), no decay to 5-, 6-, 6+.
